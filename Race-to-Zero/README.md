# Race-to-Zero | Purdue University

Race-to-Zero is a Vertically Integrated Project (VIP) at Purdue University focused on evaluating technologies and strategies that can accelerate decarbonization.

As a student researcher, I worked on projects spanning clean energy infrastructure, data-center energy efficiency, and transportation electrification. My work combined technical research with business planning, market analysis, and technology assessment.

## Projects

### 1. Small Modular Reactors for Hyperscale Data Centers

Evaluated SMR architectures for scalable, clean energy to power AI data centers.


Key areas:
- Energy reliability and scalability
- 2N+1 redundancy architecture
- System-level tradeoffs
- Data-center power requirements


### 2. Two-Phase Immersion Cooling for AI Data Centers

Evaluated advanced cooling technologies for high-density AI computing.Evaluated two-phase immersion cooling (2-PIC) as an alternative to conventional data-center cooling for high-density computing.

Key areas:
- Operating principles of two-phase cooling
- Energy efficiency
- Computing density
- Scalability
- Total cost of ownership
- Environmental impact


### 3. Decarbonizing Nautical Tourism in the Galapagos

Developed a business plan and technical assessment to transition gasoline-powered boats to electric and hydrogen propulsion.

Key areas:
- Market analysis
- Electric and hydrogen propulsion
- Operating economics
- Supply-chain considerations
- Partnerships
- Decarbonization strategy
