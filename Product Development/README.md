# Student Soybean Innovation Competition

## Overview

A multidisciplinary Purdue University project focused on developing a sustainable
soybean-derived UV-protective coating for commercial and industrial applications.

Over approximately five months, our team progressed from formulation development
and material testing to a working prototype, technical assessment, market analysis,
cost evaluation, and business plan.

## Product

We developed a UV-protective coating using 93% soybean-derived components designed
to prevent UV-A degradation of materials including wood, plastics, and plexiglass.

## What I Worked On

- Product development and formulation
- Material testing and performance evaluation
- Market and competitive research
- Cost and commercial feasibility analysis
- Business plan
- Technical assessments

## Key Outcome

The team developed a physical prototype and evaluated its potential for
commercialization within a $12B industrial coatings market, and presented our findings to industry judges.
